# CSR Outreach Portal — Quick Start Guide

A one-page guide for NGO staff. No technical knowledge needed.

## What this app does

Helps you find companies to approach for CSR partnerships, find the
right person to contact there, keep track of every company and contact
in one place, and write outreach emails faster — all from one screen at
a time.

## The main screens (top menu)

| Screen | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your homepage. Totals (companies, replies, meetings, proposals), what follow-ups are due or overdue, and a feed of recent activity. |
| **Companies** | Two tabs. **Discover companies** searches real companies across India from public sources. **Saved leads** is your own list of companies you've saved. |
| **Company Detail** | Everything about one company: info, contacts (with **Find contacts**), notes, documents, proposals, lead score, and the "Generate Email" button. |
| **Contacts** | Every contact you've saved, across all companies. Search by company, name, designation, industry, state or city; click a name for their profile (notes, timeline, source, Generate Email). |
| **Emails** | Every draft, sent email, reply, follow-up and meeting in one place. **Check for replies** looks for new answers. |
| **Settings** | Connect the NGO's Gmail account, set your sender name and signature, follow-up timing and email templates. |
| **NGO Profile** | Your organisation's own details (name, mission, focus areas, location). Fill this in once — it's used automatically when scoring leads and writing emails. |

## A typical workflow, start to finish

1. **Discover** — go to Companies → **Discover companies** and search
   by company name, industry, city, state or CSR focus (or click one of
   the example searches). Results come from public sources such as
   Wikidata and the government company registry. Nothing is added to
   your list yet.
2. **Read the badges** — next to each detail:
   - **Verified**: it comes from a named public source. Hover to see
     which; click to open it.
   - **Inferred**: the app worked it out itself, so double-check it.
   - **Unavailable**: no public source has it.

   If CSR focus says "Unavailable", click **Check CSR focus** to have
   the app read the company's own CSR web page.
3. **Save as lead** — click it on the companies worth pursuing. Only
   then is the company added to your **Saved leads**, and the lead
   score, AI tools, contacts, notes and documents become available. The
   app adds a note to the company recording where each detail came from.
   Companies you've already saved show "✓ Saved · Open lead" instead.
4. **Check the Lead Score** — every saved company shows a score (0–100) and a
   High/Medium/Low badge. Click "Why?" for a plain-language explanation.
5. **Find contacts**: click **Find contacts** on a search result, a
   saved lead, or the company's page. The app reads the company's own
   public pages (leadership, CSR, CSR Committee, contact, press) and
   lists the people to approach, most important first:
   - **Priority 1:** CSR / Sustainability / ESG / Foundation heads, the
     CSR Committee, and the CSR team.
   - **Priority 2:** HR and Corporate Communications.
   - **Priority 3:** regional and operations heads.

   Every detail has a Verified / Inferred / Unavailable badge, and each
   person has a confidence score (hover it to see why).
   - Tick **Also scan CSR report** to read the CSR Committee from the
     company's annual report. This is slower, up to a minute.
   - Save people one at a time, or tick several and click **Save
     selected**. Nothing is saved until you do.
   - If the company wasn't saved yet, saving a contact saves it as a lead
     too.
   - The panel also offers LinkedIn search links when a name isn't
     published; these open in your browser.
6. **Write and send an email**: in the company's **Emails** section,
   choose the contact and the kind of email, then click **Generate
   email**. It is saved as a draft. Read and edit it, then click
   **Review & send…**. The app shows exactly what will be sent, from
   which address, to whom. Only **Confirm & send** sends it. Tick
   "Remind me to follow up" to get a follow-up draft after 3, 7, 14 or
   30 days (you approve that one too).
   - First time: connect the NGO's Gmail in **Settings**.
   - Without Gmail you can still generate drafts and copy them.
   - Replies appear under the email with the app's reading of them
     (Positive / Negative / Neutral / Needs Follow-up, and why). Click
     **Change** if it got it wrong.
   - Set the conversation's outcome (Meeting Scheduled, Proposal Sent,
     Partnership Confirmed…) from its status menu; the company's status
     moves forward to match.
7. **Update status** — after you reach out, set the company's status
   (New → Contacted → Follow-up → Meeting → Proposal Sent → Successful /
   Not Interested) and, if relevant, a follow-up date.
8. **Add notes** — jot down anything from a call or meeting, right on
   the company's page.
9. **Track a proposal** — if you send a formal proposal, add it under
   Proposals and move it through its stages as it progresses.
10. **Upload documents** — keep the actual proposal file, a CSR report,
   or a signed MoU attached to the company.
11. **Tag companies** — add free-text tags like "High Priority" or a
   focus area, then filter by tag later to find them again.
12. **Check the Dashboard** — anytime, to see overdue follow-ups and
    what's happened recently across every company.

## A few important rules

- **Nothing is ever sent automatically.** Every email, including
  follow-ups, waits as a draft until you click Confirm & send.
- **Only public, work information is collected.** Company and contact
  details come from public sources: Wikidata, the government company
  registry, official datasets, company websites and reports.
  - Personal email addresses (gmail, yahoo, …) and mobile numbers are
    always left out, even if a page shows them.
  - The app never reads LinkedIn itself. It only gives you search links.
  - Websites that ask not to be read automatically (robots.txt) are
    skipped.
- **Discovering a company doesn't save it.** Searching never changes
  your list; only **Save as lead** does.
- **Duplicates are caught, not silently created.** If you try to add a
  company that already exists (same name or website), the app warns you
  and lets you open the existing one instead.
- **Deleting a company deletes everything under it** — its contacts,
  notes, documents, and proposals. There's no undo, so double-check
  before confirming a delete.

## Importing/exporting your existing list

On the Companies page's **Saved leads** tab: **Export** downloads your current filtered list
as a spreadsheet (CSV). **Import** lets you upload a CSV of companies —
rows that look like duplicates of what's already saved are skipped
automatically, and any row with a problem is reported to you instead of
silently failing.

## If something looks wrong

If a screen is confusing or something doesn't work as expected, note
exactly what you were trying to do and what happened — that's the most
useful thing you can hand back to whoever maintains the app.
